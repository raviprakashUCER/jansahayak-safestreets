# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary audience is citizens, commuters, students, local communities, and government officials. The initial pilot is focused on Greater Noida, Uttar Pradesh, India.

## Product Purpose

JanSahayak SafeStreets helps people document road-safety hazards, discover existing reports, find likely official reporting channels, and track the progress of civic action. It is a civic coordination and transparency layer that complements, rather than replaces, official government grievance mechanisms.

## Positioning

JanSahayak makes road hazards visible, follow-up traceable, and public safety a shared responsibility by combining structured, location-aware reporting with discoverable cases and clear status histories.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Confirmed capabilities include structured hazard reporting, a public safety map, duplicate-report suggestions, authority-directory guidance, case tracking, evidence and activity timelines, escalation records, notifications, moderation, and analytics. AI assistance must be clearly distinguished from verified information and official updates.

The public landing page should explain the service and lead users to report a hazard or explore the map. The product must not imply that it is a government authority or replace official grievance channels. Pilot targets and proposed metrics in the reference are not current performance claims.

## Evidence on Hand

No product evidence, testimonials, case studies, or proof assets have been confirmed. Future work must not fabricate them.

## Product Principles

- Make road hazards easy to document.
- Make civic follow-up understandable and traceable.
- Help people find the right official path without overstating certainty.
- Distinguish citizen reports, AI suggestions, verified information, and official updates.
- Build public trust through useful transparency, not fear or inflated claims.
